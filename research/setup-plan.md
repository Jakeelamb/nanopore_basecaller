## Scope and acceptance

Use approximately 2 to 8 GB of raw signals, one full GRCh38 reference, a vendor binary plus matching source, and two model tiers. Preserve at least 30 GiB free. This is a pilot, not whole-genome coverage or a powered evaluation. Setup runs may establish software compatibility but cannot demonstrate an improvement.

Acquisition records identify the URL, source object size and checksum when provided, local SHA-256, retrieval time, and intended use. Original files remain separate from derived results. Independent sample or flowcell partitions take precedence over random read splits. The subsequent authorization permits stock processing of the test control; candidate selection remains restricted to development and validation.

## Throughput checkpoint

Check download rate, remaining storage, and GPU compatibility after the first binary and raw object. Do not acquire complete flowcell archives. If the estimate exceeds a multi-hour run or the disk reserve, reduce the pilot while retaining its scientific limits in the report.

## Structural choice

Use a flat asset manifest and command-line scripts rather than an experiment framework. Assets are immutable inputs identified by hashes. Run directories contain commands, UTC clocks, exit status, stdout, and stderr. Acquisition used a direct workflow. The extended benchmark request received two independent design candidates and an independent judgment, retained under `provenance/design/`.
